# Agora — Current Pricing Snapshot

## IMPORTANT
Pricing is time-sensitive. This document reflects the official Agora pricing pages checked in September 2026. The sales agent should retrieve the latest pricing source before quoting prices in production.

## Conversational AI Platform
Agora's current pricing page states:
- Conversational AI Engine Audio Task: $0.10 per minute
- First 300 minutes free
- The listed unit price includes usage of selected ASR, LLM and TTS models.
- Agora states that for voice AI agents, pricing can include SIP/PSTN and is based on answered duration, rounded up to the next full minute.
- Agent Studio is included as part of the platform offering.

## Sales rule
Never invent a discount or custom enterprise price.

If the customer asks:
"Can you give me 20% off?"

The agent should not claim approval. It should say that custom pricing / volume discounts may be available and that the exact offer depends on the customer's requirements and commercial agreement.
