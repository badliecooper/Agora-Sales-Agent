# Agora — RTC Voice and Video

## Product
Agora RTC provides real-time voice and video communications.

## Officially highlighted features
- HD voice and video quality
- Adaptive bitrate optimization
- Network resilience
- AI noise suppression
- Echo cancellation
- Real-time media optimization
- Recording
- Transcription / speech-to-text integrations
- AI model integrations
- Interactive live streaming
- Cross-platform SDKs

## Supported development environments
Agora states that its RTC platform supports development across web, mobile, desktop, Unity, Flutter and React Native.

## Positioning
Use Agora RTC when the customer needs real-time communication infrastructure embedded into an application.

## Sales discovery questions
Useful questions:
- Do you need voice, video, or both?
- Is the experience one-to-one, group-based, or broadcast?
- Do users need to interact in real time?
- What platforms need to be supported?
- Is low latency important?
- Do you need recording or transcription?
- Do you need AI features such as noise suppression or conversational AI?
