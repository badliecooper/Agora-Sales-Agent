# Enterprise Pricing & Licensing Guide

## Overview
Our conversational AI sales platform offers transparent, predictable pricing models tailored for high-growth startups to Fortune 500 enterprises. All plans feature ultra-low latency voice pipelines (sub-500ms) powered by Agora RTC.

## Subscription Tiers

### 1. Starter Tier ($499 / month)
- Up to 1,500 conversational voice minutes per month.
- Standard RAG knowledge base search with up to 100 documents.
- Web browser voice interface widget.
- Standard email support with 24-hour response SLA.
- Standard speech-to-text (STT) and text-to-speech (TTS) voices.

### 2. Growth Tier ($1,499 / month)
- Up to 5,000 conversational voice minutes per month.
- Up to 1,000 documents with real-time vector indexing.
- CRM integrations: HubSpot, Salesforce, and Webhook dispatchers.
- Real-time agent interruption handling and sentiment analysis.
- Dedicated Slack support channel and 4-hour business SLA.

### 3. Enterprise Custom Tier (Starting at $3,500 / month)
- Unlimited concurrent voice channels.
- Custom vector partition with tenant-isolated Pinecone namespaces.
- Custom fine-tuned voice models and zero-latency prompt injection.
- Dedicated account manager and 99.99% uptime guarantee.
- SOC 2 Type II compliance, HIPAA compliance agreement (BAA), and SSO/SAML 2.0.

## Volume Overage Rates
- Starter: $0.35 per additional voice minute.
- Growth: $0.25 per additional voice minute.
- Enterprise: $0.15 per additional voice minute (billed monthly in arrears).

## Annual Discounts
- 20% discount on upfront annual commitments across all tiers.
- Waived onboarding and CRM configuration fee ($2,500 value) for annual Enterprise contracts.
