# Sales Objection Handling Playbook

## Objection 1: "We already use an IVR / automated phone tree."
- **Sales Response**: "Traditional IVRs force customers through rigid, frustrating number menus ('Press 1 for Sales, Press 2 for Support'). Our Agora-powered AI voice agent speaks with natural human conversational flow. It understands complex customer intent, answers nuanced questions instantly using your private knowledge base, and only escalates to human reps when high-value human touch is needed."
- **Key Metric**: Decreases call abandon rates by 68% and improves customer satisfaction (CSAT) by 42%.

---

## Objection 2: "Is our company data secure and isolated?"
- **Sales Response**: "Security and strict multi-tenancy are our core architectural pillars. Every single vector stored in our knowledge base carries a cryptographically enforced company isolation filter. No query executed by another company can ever see, search, or index your proprietary documents or pricing."
- **Compliance Standards**: SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA BAA available on Enterprise plans, end-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption, and data-at-rest encryption via AWS KMS / AES-256.

---

## Objection 3: "How does it handle interruptions if the prospect interrupts mid-sentence?"
- **Sales Response**: "Unlike clunky voicebots that talk over the caller until their scripted audio finishes, our agent uses Agora Real-Time Voice Activity Detection (VAD). The millisecond a customer speaks or interrupts, audio output instantly cuts off and the agent shifts immediately into listening mode."

---

## Objection 4: "Can we integrate this with our existing CRM?"
- **Sales Response**: "Yes. We offer native out-of-the-box integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, and custom REST webhooks. As soon as a call concludes, the agent summarizes the conversation, extracts qualification criteria (budget, authority, need, timeline), and automatically creates or updates the CRM contact and deal record."
