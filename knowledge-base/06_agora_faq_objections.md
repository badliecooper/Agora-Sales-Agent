# Agora — Sales FAQ and Objection Handling

## Q: What is Agora?
A: Agora provides real-time engagement infrastructure for voice, video, chat, streaming, collaboration and conversational AI.

## Q: Can Agora be used for AI voice agents?
A: Yes. Agora currently offers a Conversational AI Engine positioned for building real-time voice AI agents.

## Q: Do I have to use one specific LLM?
A: Agora positions its Conversational AI Engine as flexible, allowing integration with different AI models/LLMs. Verify specific model support in current documentation.

## Q: Can Agora handle interruptions?
A: Agora's Conversational AI Engine highlights intelligent interruption handling so the AI can stop speaking when it detects user interruption.

## Q: Can Agora handle background noise?
A: Agora offers AI Noise Suppression and states that its Conversational AI platform includes noise suppression and echo cancellation.

## Q: Can I build voice and video?
A: Yes. Agora RTC supports real-time voice and video communication.

## Q: Can I build on the web?
A: Agora provides web support as part of its cross-platform RTC development approach.

## Q: Can Agora integrate with AI models?
A: Agora highlights AI integrations and a Conversational AI Engine that can connect with LLM, ASR and TTS components.

## Q: Is Agora only for calling?
A: No. Agora's portfolio includes RTC, conversational AI, chat, signaling, live streaming, media services and AI extensions.

## Q: How much does Agora cost?
A: Pricing depends on the product and usage. Current official pricing should be retrieved before quoting.

## Objection: "Agora is just another video calling SDK."
Response strategy:
Clarify that Agora now positions its platform more broadly around real-time engagement, including voice AI and conversational AI, in addition to RTC.

## Objection: "We already have an LLM."
Response strategy:
Explain that the value proposition can be the real-time voice infrastructure, low-latency communication and voice interaction capabilities around the model.

## Objection: "We need natural conversations."
Response strategy:
Discuss interruption handling, noise suppression, echo cancellation and low-latency real-time interaction.

## Objection: "We need to scale."
Response strategy:
Explain that Agora positions its real-time network and platform for global, scalable real-time experiences. Do not quote a specific concurrency guarantee without current documentation.
